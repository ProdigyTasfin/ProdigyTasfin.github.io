About Me

My name is Tasfin Hasan Sakib. I'm a computer science student with a deep curiosity for building things that help people. I believe technology should not only entertain us — it should help us focus, learn, relax, and live more intentionally.

I build apps that solve problems I experience myself. I test them in my own daily routine, and then share them with others who may be facing the same challenges.

This curiosity keeps me building. I am continuously learning, experimenting, and creating new apps that I hope will genuinely help people.

Why I Build

Life can sometimes feel overwhelming — full of distractions, monotony, and pressure. I've experienced those moments too. That's why I started building tools to help me navigate my own days more intentionally.

Every app I create starts with a personal need:

  • Reducing distractions so I can focus on what matters
  • Structuring my time so I can study and code without burnout
  • Breaking monotony so I can stay engaged with everyday tasks

I share these tools because I know others face similar challenges. My goal is to build apps that make a real difference in people's daily lives.

Apps I've Built

Noctra

Digital wellbeing

Noctra helps reduce distractions, control excessive social media use, and build healthier digital habits. I personally use it when I need to stay away from distracting apps and focus on important work.

Temvica

Pomodoro timer

Temvica helps me stay focused while studying and coding. I use its 25‑minute focus sessions followed by 5‑minute breaks to work without becoming overwhelmed.

HushFlow

Background audio

HushFlow is one of my favorite projects. It helps make repetitive or tiring tasks feel less monotonous through music, podcasts, and background audio.

I'm always working on new ideas. If you have a suggestion, feel free to reach out!
