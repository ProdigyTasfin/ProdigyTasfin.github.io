How to play music with the screen off on Android

A simple workflow to keep your audio playing after you lock your device.

  1. Start music or a podcast in HushFlow.
  2. Confirm playback has started — check that a media notification appears.
  3. Lock your phone or turn the display off.
  4. Playback continues in the background where Android allows it.
  5. Use supported controls from the notification panel or lock screen.
Important: HushFlow supports playback when the screen is off or the phone is locked. If the Android device itself is completely powered down, applications cannot continue running — this is a limitation of the operating system, not the app.

Screen off vs. phone powered off — what's the difference?

This distinction matters for understanding how HushFlow works.

When many people say "phone off," they mean the screen is off or the device is locked. HushFlow can continue playing audio in both of these states.

However, if an Android phone is completely powered down — turned off entirely — no app can continue running. This is a fundamental limitation of the Android operating system.

HushFlow is designed to support the common use case of keeping audio playing while the screen is off, so you can listen without draining your battery on a bright display.
