Why use HushFlow?

HushFlow is designed for moments when you want to keep listening without being tied to your phone screen.

  • Listening to music or podcasts while studying or working
  • Playing audio while using another app, such as maps or messaging
  • Keeping audio playing with the phone screen locked
  • Setting a sleep timer before bed
  • Organising a temporary queue of tracks
  • Adjusting sound with equaliser controls

HushFlow focuses on the basics that matter for background listening.

It is not a streaming service and does not host or provide access to copyrighted content. It plays audio you already have access to through your device or supported sources.

Features for background listening and multitasking

HushFlow is built around practical audio tools: background playback, queue management, playlists, sleep timer, and sound customisation.

Background and screen‑off playback

Supported audio can continue playing while you use other apps, minimise HushFlow, or lock the screen — ideal for studying, working, or relaxing.

Playback controls

Control playback from notifications, the lock screen, or the in‑app mini player. Use play, pause, skip, and repeat controls.

Queue management

Add tracks to the queue, reorder them, remove items, and manage what plays next. Simple queue controls keep your listening flow organised.

Playlists

Create and manage custom playlists where supported. Organise your favourite tracks for quick access.

Sleep timer

Set a countdown timer to stop playback automatically. Optionally stop after the current track — useful for bedtime or relaxation sessions.

Audio discovery

Browse categories including Focus, Sleep, Mood, Pop, and Jazz to find audio that fits your activity.

Audio Lab

Adjust sound with a 10‑band equaliser, bass boost, virtualizer, loudness enhancer, presets, and saved preferences.

Audio effects may vary by Android device, OS version, and connected audio output.

How HushFlow works

Getting started with background listening is straightforward.

  1. Open HushFlow and choose supported audio from your device or available sources.
  2. Start playback and manage your queue or playlists as you go.
  3. Continue listening while using other apps or locking the screen — playback continues in the background.
  4. Use extra controls like the sleep timer, equaliser, or saved presets when you need them.

Who HushFlow is for

HushFlow is designed for anyone who wants flexible background audio control on Android.

  • Students – listen while studying without needing to keep the screen on.
  • Remote workers and developers – keep audio playing while you focus on other tasks.
  • People who listen during repetitive tasks – make routine activities more enjoyable with background audio.
  • Users who want screen‑off playback – save battery and reduce distractions.
  • Anyone who uses a sleep timer – fall asleep to audio without worrying about it playing all night.

HushFlow is a tool, not a service.

It does not provide music or audio content itself. It plays audio you already have access to through your device or other sources, and gives you better control over how you listen.

Frequently asked questions

What is HushFlow?

HushFlow is an Android background audio player with screen‑off playback, queue controls, playlists, a sleep timer, and sound customisation.

Can HushFlow play while the screen is off?

HushFlow is designed to continue supported audio playback while the device screen is locked, subject to Android device and system limitations.

Can I use other apps while HushFlow is playing?

Yes. HushFlow supports background playback, so you can use other apps while audio continues to play.
