HushFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

HushFlow respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information while providing audio playback, Premium features, and app functionality.

Information We Collect

HushFlow may process limited technical information required for:

  • Audio playback functionality
  • Premium purchase verification
  • Rewarded advertisements
  • Crash reporting and stability improvements

HushFlow does not sell personal information.

Google Play Billing

Premium purchases are securely processed through Google Play Billing. HushFlow does not directly store payment card information.

Advertisements

HushFlow may display rewarded advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob.

Background Playback

HushFlow may continue audio playback while the app is minimized or the device screen is off, depending on device settings and Android system behavior.

Third-Party Services

HushFlow may use Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time to reflect app improvements or policy changes. The Last updated date will change when updates are published.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact nextflowapps@gmail.com.
