Our mission

Humans first.

Technology reaches people across different languages, beliefs, incomes, cultures, abilities, and lives. We don't believe any of those things make one user more important than another.

Wherever you are, if you're using something we've built, you deserve a product made with care.

Everyone matters.
Why we build

Technology should work for people.

Technology can save time. It can teach. It can entertain. It can remove friction. It can help someone focus. It can make an ordinary day slightly easier.

It can also waste time, manipulate behaviour, create dependency that wasn't asked for, and treat people like metrics on a dashboard.

We want to build more of the first kind.

We're not against technology. We're against technology that forgets the person using it.
How we work

A few things we try to hold to.

Not a manifesto. Just the rules we keep coming back to.

01

Humans first

Start with the person, not the metric. Every decision we make has to make sense from their side of the screen.

02

Useful before impressive

A product should solve something real before it tries to look clever. Real problems first, aesthetics second.

03

Respect attention

We don't design unnecessary addiction into products. No dark patterns, no fake urgency, no engagement mechanics that don't serve the user.

04

Accessible by default

Everyone should be able to use the core experience of our products, regardless of where they are or what they can afford.

05

Privacy with purpose

Collect only what a feature genuinely needs. Prefer on-device processing where it's practical and honest.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is NextFlow Apps?

NextFlow Apps is a small independent team building thoughtful, accessible software for real human problems. We're not limited to any one category — the products we ship today just happen to be about attention, focus, and listening.

What does "Humans first" mean?

It means we start from the person using the product, not the metric we could extract from them. Where someone is from, what they believe, what they look like, or how much they earn doesn't change how much they matter to us.

Who builds NextFlow Apps?

A small team of people working across product thinking, engineering, design, and testing. Tasfin Hasan Sakib currently leads much of the product direction and development. Other team members contribute behind the scenes and prefer not to be named publicly.

Are the apps free?

Yes. The core experience of every NextFlow app is free to use, and some apps offer optional premium features for people who want more.

Which products exist today?

HushFlow for listening, Noctra for digital wellbeing and app blocking, and Temvica for focus and Pomodoro sessions. All three are available on Google Play.

Are you building more?

Yes — we're actively exploring new products. We don't announce them until they're ready, and we don't want to over-promise. When something is ready, it'll show up here first.

How can I suggest an app idea or report an issue?

Send ideas, feedback, or bug reports to nextflowapps@gmail.com or message @nextflow_apps on Instagram. We read every message.

Where can I read the legal policies?

Our Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, and Terms & Conditions are on this website. Each app also has its own privacy policy on its landing page.
