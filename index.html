Apps built around real everyday problems

Each app is designed to address a specific challenge you might face in your daily digital life.

Background listening

HushFlow supports users who want audio to continue while multitasking, locking the screen or using a sleep timer — without needing to keep the app open.

Healthier screen habits

Noctra supports users who want stronger boundaries around distracting apps, endless scrolling and mindless phone use — with practical tools to interrupt automatic habits.

Structured focus sessions

Temvica supports users who prefer timed work, tasks, ambient sounds and visible progress — a simple framework for focused study or deep work.
