How Temvica works

A simple process to help you focus, one session at a time.

  1. Create or choose one task — give your focus session a clear purpose.
  2. Select a focus mode or custom duration — choose from Focus, Flow, Deep, Rest, or set your own timings.
  3. Start the timer — optionally play an ambient sound to help you settle into the session.
  4. Complete the session and take a break — rest, then continue or start a new task.
  5. Review your progress — see completed sessions, focused minutes, and patterns over time.

Temvica is one approach to focus, not a guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

What is Temvica?

Temvica is an Android Pomodoro focus timer that combines timed focus sessions, task planning, ambient sounds, and productivity insights to help you study and work more effectively.

How does the Pomodoro timer work?

Temvica lets you set focus and break durations. You work during the focus period, then take a short break. This cycle can be repeated throughout your work or study session.

What focus modes are available?

Temvica includes Focus, Flow, Deep, and Rest modes, along with the ability to set custom focus and break durations.

Can I create tasks in Temvica?

Yes. You can quickly create tasks, organise them into study, work, or personal categories, and link each task to a focus session.

Can I link a task to a focus session?

Yes. Choose a task before starting a Pomodoro session to keep your work aligned with your chosen focus.

Does Temvica include ambient sounds?

Yes. Temvica includes rain, cafe, fan, and keyboard sounds. Premium users may also access custom device audio.

Does Temvica work offline?

Yes. Temvica supports offline timer functionality. Your tasks, focus sessions, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Is Temvica free?

Temvica offers a free experience with core Pomodoro features, task management, ambient sounds, and basic insights. Premium features are available via subscription or lifetime purchase.

What may be included with Temvica Premium?

Premium may include timer styles, custom timers, detailed insights, custom device audio, additional personalisation, and lifetime Premium support. Availability may vary.

What permissions may Temvica request?

Temvica may request notification permission for timer alerts. It may request file or media access only when you choose custom device audio, depending on your Android version.

How do I delete my Temvica data?

You can delete your data by clearing the app storage via Android Settings, uninstalling the app, or using the in-app deletion controls where available.
