How to use the Pomodoro technique for studying

A simple, proven way to structure study sessions. Choose a task, focus for a timed interval, take a break, and repeat.

The Pomodoro study method is one of the most effective ways to maintain concentration during study sessions. Instead of trying to study for hours without interruption, you commit to a short, focused block — traditionally 25 minutes — followed by a brief break.

Here's how it works with Temvica:

  1. Choose a study task — pick one topic, assignment, or chapter.
  2. Set your focus duration — start with 25 minutes, or adjust to suit your concentration.
  3. Focus without switching — work only on that task until the timer ends.
  4. Take a short break — 5 minutes to rest and reset.
  5. Repeat — after several sessions, take a longer break.

Temvica's Focus, Flow, Deep, and Rest modes let you match the timer to the kind of study you're doing — whether it's reading, memorisation, problem-solving, or deep work.

Find your own rhythm. The Pomodoro technique is flexible. Some students prefer 45-minute sessions for mathematics or coding. Experiment to see what works for you.
