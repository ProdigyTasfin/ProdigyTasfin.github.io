Frequently asked questions

What is Temvica?

Temvica is a Pomodoro timer and focus tool for Android. It combines structured focus sessions, task management, ambient soundscapes, and productivity insights to help you study and work more effectively.

Is Temvica available on Google Play?

Yes. Temvica is publicly available for Android through Google Play.

View Temvica on Google Play

Is Temvica free?

Temvica offers a free experience with core Pomodoro features, task management, ambient sounds, and basic insights. Premium features are available via subscription or lifetime purchase.

Does Temvica work offline?

Yes. Temvica supports offline timer functionality. Your tasks, focus sessions, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

How do I delete my Temvica data?

You can delete your data by clearing the app storage via Android Settings, uninstalling the app, or using the in-app deletion controls where available.

View full data deletion instructions

What permissions does Temvica require?

Temvica may request notification permission for timer alerts and storage permission if you choose to use custom audio files. All permissions are optional.

What devices support Temvica?

Temvica is designed for Android devices running Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) and above. Performance may vary depending on device capabilities.
