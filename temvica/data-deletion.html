Data deletion

Delete your Temvica data

Temvica does not currently require or provide user accounts. Most Temvica data is stored locally on your Android device.

Last updated: July 2026

Method 1: Delete data inside Temvica

You can delete individual tasks, session history, or other saved information directly within the app where these controls are available.

  • Open Temvica and navigate to the relevant section (Tasks, History, or Settings).
  • Use the delete, clear, or reset options provided.
  • Confirm your action when prompted.

Note: Not all data types may have in-app deletion controls. If you cannot find the option, use Method 2 or 3 below.

Method 2: Clear app storage (Android Settings)

This method removes all locally stored Temvica data, including tasks, focus history, preferences, and settings.

  1. Open Android Settings on your device.
  2. Select Apps or Application Manager.
  3. Find and select Temvica from the list.
  4. Select Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage or Clear data.
  6. Confirm your action when prompted.
⚠️ Important: This action is permanent. All tasks, focus sessions, statistics, and settings will be deleted and cannot be recovered.

Method 3: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Temvica removes all locally stored application data from your device. This is the most thorough way to delete all Temvica data.

  1. Long-press the Temvica app icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  2. Select Uninstall (or drag to the uninstall option).
  3. Confirm your action when prompted.

Alternatively, you can uninstall via Android Settings → Apps → Temvica → Uninstall.

If you reinstall Temvica later, it will start fresh with no previous data.

Request deletion of support information

If you have contacted us via email and would like us to delete your support communication or any personal information we may have received, you can submit a formal deletion request.

How to request deletion

Email nextflowapps@gmail.com using the required subject line:

  • Subject: Temvica Data Deletion Request

In your email, please include:

  • The email address you used when contacting us (if applicable)
  • A brief description of the support communication or information you want deleted
  • Any reference numbers or dates that could help us locate the communication

What happens next

  • We will verify your request and may ask for additional details to locate the communication.
  • Once verified, we will delete the requested information from our email records.
  • We will confirm the deletion via email.
  • Requests are typically handled within 30 days.
📌 Important exceptions: Certain records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate record‑keeping purposes (e.g., transaction receipts, legal obligations). We will inform you if any part of your request cannot be fulfilled due to such requirements.

Google Play data

Google Play purchases, subscriptions, payment records, and advertising data are controlled by Google. To delete or manage that data, please refer to your Google Account settings or contact Google Support.
