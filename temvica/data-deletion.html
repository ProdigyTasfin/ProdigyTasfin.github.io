Data deletion

Delete your Temvica data

Temvica does not currently require or provide user accounts. Most Temvica data is stored locally on your Android device.

Method 1: Delete data inside Temvica

Delete individual tasks, session history or other saved information where these controls are available.

Method 2: Clear app storage

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Select Apps.
  3. Select Temvica.
  4. Select Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage or Clear data.

Method 3: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Temvica removes locally stored application data from that device.

Request deletion of support information

Email nextflowapps@gmail.com using the required subject Temvica Data Deletion Request. Please describe the support communication or information you want deleted.

The developer may request limited details needed to locate the communication. Requests will be handled within a reasonable period. Certain records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or legitimate record-keeping.

Google Play purchases, subscriptions, payment records and advertising data are controlled by Google and may need to be managed through your Google account or Google support.
