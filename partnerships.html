A focused product portfolio.

Our products address distinct but connected needs: protecting attention, structuring focused work, and making audio useful throughout the day. This creates opportunities for shared technology, cross-product learning, and thoughtful growth.

Noctra

Digital wellbeing tools that help people set practical boundaries around distracting apps and browsing.

Temvica

Pomodoro sessions, tasks, and ambient audio designed for study, creative work, and sustained focus.

HushFlow

Flexible background audio for music, podcasts, learning, relaxation, and screen-off listening.
