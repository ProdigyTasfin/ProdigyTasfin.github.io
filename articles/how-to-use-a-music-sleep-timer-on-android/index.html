How to Set a Music Sleep Timer on Android

Set a timer to stop music, podcasts or ambient audio automatically on Android. Learn where to find sleep-timer settings and what to do if your app has none.

By NextFlow Apps Published Jul 10, 2026 Updated October 7, 2026 5 min read

To set a music sleep timer on Android, start playing audio, open the player’s menu or playback settings, select Sleep Timer, choose a duration such as 15, 30, or 60 minutes, and confirm it. The exact steps vary by app because Android does not provide one universal sleep timer for every music and podcast player.

How to Set a Timer to Stop Music on Android

Follow these general steps to set a sleep timer in your music or podcast app:

  1. Open the music, podcast, or audio app you want to use.
  2. Start playing the audio you want to listen to.
  3. Open the playback menu, three‑dot menu, or settings – often found in the full player view.
  4. Select Sleep Timer, Timer, Stop after, or a similar option.
  5. Choose a duration – for example, 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
  6. Select end‑of‑track or end‑of‑episode where available.
  7. Confirm the timer – many apps show the remaining time beside a clock icon.
  8. Lock the phone and verify that playback stops at the chosen time.
Note: Exact wording and menu placement depend on the app you are using. If you cannot find the timer, check the app’s settings or help section.

What Is a Music Sleep Timer?

A music sleep timer is a playback control that automatically stops or pauses audio after a selected amount of time. It is designed for users who want to fall asleep to music, podcasts, audiobooks, or ambient sounds without the audio continuing all night.

  • It prevents playback from running through the night.
  • Some apps offer end‑of‑track or end‑of‑episode options.
  • It may work while the screen is locked if the audio app supports background playback.

Can Android Automatically Stop Music?

Android does not provide one guaranteed, universal sleep timer for every audio app. However, many music and podcast apps include their own built‑in sleep timer.

  • A built‑in player timer is usually the most reliable method.
  • Some phone automation tools may stop or pause media, but support varies.
  • Clock‑app workarounds differ by manufacturer and Android version.

How to Stop Music After 30 Minutes

If your app has a sleep timer, stopping music after 30 minutes is straightforward:

  1. Start playback.
  2. Open the sleep timer.
  3. Choose 30 minutes.
  4. Confirm the timer.
  5. Lock the screen if desired – the timer should usually continue counting while the phone is locked.

How to Stop Music at the End of a Track

Some apps offer an end‑of‑track option. This is useful when you want the current song or audio track to finish naturally before stopping. Not every app provides this option.

How to Stop a Podcast at the End of an Episode

Some podcast players offer an end‑of‑episode option. This prevents the next episode from starting automatically. Look for this option inside the timer menu or playback settings.

Does a Sleep Timer Work With the Screen Off?

Usually yes, when the app supports background playback. The timer belongs to the audio app and can continue while the phone is locked. However, battery restrictions may interrupt playback before the timer ends. Notification and background permissions may be necessary, and manufacturer settings can affect reliability.

For more detail on keeping audio playing with the screen off, see our guide: how to play audio with the screen off on Android.

What If My Music App Has No Sleep Timer?

If your app does not have a built‑in sleep timer, try these options:

  • Use a player with a built‑in sleep timer – several dedicated music, podcast, and ambient‑sound apps include this feature.
  • Create a short playlist or limited queue that ends naturally.
  • Disable autoplay so playback stops after the current queue.
  • Use an end‑of‑track option if available.
  • Use a trusted third‑party timer app carefully – review its permissions and privacy policy first.
  • Use device automation only when supported – be aware that setup and compatibility vary.
Important: Do not attempt to bypass another app’s restrictions or use a third‑party tool to control audio in ways the original app does not permit.

What Sleep‑Timer Duration Should You Use?

Here are some practical starting points:

  • 10–15 minutes – short relaxation
  • 20–30 minutes – falling asleep
  • 45–60 minutes – longer podcasts or ambient audio
  • End‑of‑track – for music
  • End‑of‑episode – for podcasts

These are practical starting points, not medical recommendations. Adjust based on your own sleep routine.

Why Did My Music Sleep Timer Not Work?

Here are common causes and how to fix them:

  • Timer was not confirmed: Some apps require a confirm button after selecting a duration.
  • App was force‑closed: Swiping the app away from recent apps can stop background activity.
  • Battery optimization stopped the app: Aggressive power management may prevent background timers from firing. Check battery settings.
  • Autoplay started another track: Disable autoplay if the timer is set for a single track or episode.
  • Timer was set to end‑of‑track instead of a duration: Ensure the correct mode is selected.
  • App update changed the menu: The timer may have moved – check the app’s settings or help section.
  • Background playback is restricted: Allow background activity for the app in Android settings.
  • Notification permission is disabled: Media notifications may be required for the timer to work.
  • Third‑party timer lacks necessary permission: Review its permissions and privacy policy.
  • Bluetooth or casting behavior changed playback: Test without Bluetooth or casting.
  • The app does not support the selected timer mode: Check the app’s documentation.

Troubleshooting Checklist

  1. Confirm the timer is active – check for a visible timer indicator.
  2. Check the remaining timer duration.
  3. Disable autoplay if needed.
  4. Allow background playback in Android settings.
  5. Allow app notifications.
  6. Remove aggressive battery restrictions.
  7. Update the app.
  8. Test with the phone locked.
  9. Test without Bluetooth or casting.
  10. Restart the app.
  11. Try a built‑in timer instead of a third‑party workaround.
Pro tip

Change one setting at a time and test the timer after each change. This helps you identify exactly what resolved the issue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set a timer to stop music on Android?

Open your music or podcast app, start playback, look for a sleep timer option in the player menu, choose a duration, and confirm it. The exact steps depend on your app.

Can I stop music automatically after 30 minutes?

Yes, if your audio app includes a sleep timer. Start playback, open the sleep timer settings, choose 30 minutes, and confirm. The timer will stop playback after that period.

Does a sleep timer work when the screen is off?

Generally yes, when the app supports background playback. The timer runs in the background and stops playback even while the screen is locked.

Can a sleep timer stop podcasts?

Yes. Some podcast apps offer duration-based timers or end-of-episode options. Check your app's playback menu or settings.

Does Android have a universal music sleep timer?

No. Android does not provide one built-in timer that works for every audio app. Sleep timers are usually included inside individual music and podcast apps.

What happens if my music app has no sleep timer?

You can use a shorter playlist, disable autoplay, use a dedicated audio app with a timer, or, with caution, explore third-party timer apps. Check permissions and privacy policies before using third-party tools.

Why did my timer not stop the music?

Common reasons include the timer not being confirmed, battery restrictions, autoplay starting a new track, or the app being force-closed. Review the troubleshooting section for specific fixes.

Does HushFlow include a sleep timer?

Yes. HushFlow includes a built-in sleep timer that stops playback after a selected duration. It also supports screen-off playback and lock-screen controls.

Final Thoughts

A music sleep timer is a practical feature for anyone who enjoys falling asleep to audio. It stops playback automatically, saves battery, and removes the worry of audio playing all night.

Remember that the timer is usually provided by the audio app itself, not by Android system settings. Start by locating the timer in your preferred app, set a short test duration, and confirm that it works with screen-off playback.

One action to try today: Open your audio app, start permitted audio, set a 5‑minute test timer, lock the screen, and confirm that playback stops correctly. Once it works, you can use a longer timer for bedtime.

If you have ongoing sleep difficulties, consider speaking with a qualified professional. A sleep timer is a useful tool, but it is not a treatment for sleep disorders.
