How to Play Music With the Screen Off on Android

Learn how to keep music, podcasts and audio playing when your Android screen is off. Fix battery restrictions, background playback and lock-screen controls.

By Tasfin Hasan Sakib Published Jul 12, 2026 Updated Jul 29, 2026 5 min read

Android can play music while the screen is off when the audio app supports background playback. Start playing the audio, confirm that media controls appear in the notification area, and then lock the phone. If playback stops, battery optimization, background activity, notification permissions, or manufacturer restrictions may be blocking the app.

How to Play Music With the Screen Off on Android

  1. Open an audio app that supports background playback.
  2. Start playing music, a podcast, or another supported audio source.
  3. Confirm that media controls appear in the notification panel.
  4. Lock the phone.
  5. Use notification or lock-screen controls.
  6. Change battery and background settings if playback stops.
Note: Exact settings can vary by phone manufacturer and Android version.

How Background Playback Works

  • The app continues running as a background media service.
  • Android displays media controls through notifications and the lock screen.
  • Battery-saving systems may stop apps that are not allowed to run in the background.
  • Not every browser, video source, or media app supports screen-off playback.

How to Keep Music Playing When Your Phone Is Locked

To keep music playing when the screen is off, you need:

  • An audio app that supports background playback
  • Background activity permission enabled
  • A visible media notification
  • Notification permission enabled
  • Battery optimization exemption for the audio app (if needed)
  • A stable internet connection for streaming
  • A working Bluetooth connection (if using wireless headphones)

Why Does Music Stop When I Lock My Phone?

  • Battery optimization: The system may stop background apps to save power. Solution: set the app to "Unrestricted" or "Allow background activity."
  • Restricted background activity: The app may be restricted from running in the background. Solution: allow background activity in app settings.
  • Missing notification permission: Without a media notification, the app may be paused. Solution: enable notifications for the app.
  • Aggressive manufacturer battery management: Some brands (Samsung, Xiaomi, etc.) have additional battery-saving features. Solution: adjust manufacturer-specific settings.
  • Force-stopped app: If you force-stop the app, it won't play in the background. Solution: restart the app.
  • Mobile data restriction: The app may be restricted from using mobile data in the background. Solution: allow background data.
  • Data Saver restriction: Data Saver may limit background data. Solution: allow the app to use data in the background.
  • Unsupported media source: Some websites or media players don't support background playback. Solution: use a dedicated audio app.
  • Browser limitation: Browsers often stop audio when the screen is off. Solution: use an app designed for audio.
  • App crash or playback bug: The app may have a bug. Solution: update the app.
  • Bluetooth disconnection: Audio may pause if Bluetooth disconnects. Solution: reconnect.
  • Low-memory app termination: The system may terminate apps to free up memory. Solution: close other apps.

How to Allow Background Music on Different Android Phones

Menu names can vary by Android version and manufacturer skin. Below are common paths for popular brands.

Samsung Galaxy

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select the audio app.
  4. Tap Battery.
  5. Select Unrestricted.

Also check: Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Never sleeping apps. Add the audio app to the list.

Note: Names may vary across One UI versions.

Xiaomi and Redmi

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select Manage apps.
  4. Choose the audio app.
  5. Open Battery saver.
  6. Select No restrictions.

Also enable Autostart where available.

Note: Menus differ between MIUI and HyperOS.

Google Pixel

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select the audio app.
  4. Open App battery usage.
  5. Allow background usage.

Note: The exact wording may differ by Android version.

OnePlus

Allow background activity for the audio app in Settings → Apps → select the app → Battery → Allow background activity.

Oppo and Realme

Allow background activity, disable battery optimization, and enable automatic launch for the audio app in Settings.

Why Are Lock-Screen Music Controls Missing?

If lock-screen controls are missing, check:

  • Notification permission is enabled for the app.
  • Media notifications are not hidden.
  • Lock-screen notifications are allowed.
  • The app is using a supported media session.
  • Battery restrictions are not stopping the app.
  • Manufacturer-specific lock-screen settings are configured.

Practical steps:

  1. Allow notifications for the app.
  2. Allow lock-screen notifications.
  3. Start playback before locking the phone.
  4. Remove battery restrictions.
  5. Restart the app.
  6. Restart the device if necessary.

Does Playing Music With the Screen Off Save Battery?

Turning off the display usually reduces battery consumption compared to keeping the screen on. However:

  • Streaming still uses Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • Bluetooth still consumes power.
  • High volume and audio processing can increase consumption.
  • Downloaded audio may use less network power than streaming.
  • Battery savings vary by device, app, connection, and battery condition.

There are no fixed percentage savings — it depends on your specific usage.

Frequently asked questions

Can Android play music with the screen off?

Yes. Android can play music with the screen off when the app supports background playback and Android allows it to continue running.

Why does music stop when I lock my phone?

Common causes include battery optimization, background restrictions, missing notifications, or unsupported playback. Check the troubleshooting section above.

How do I allow background music on Samsung?

Go to Settings → Apps → select the audio app → Battery → select Unrestricted. Also check Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Never sleeping apps. Menu names may vary.

How do I keep music playing on Xiaomi or Redmi?

Go to Settings → Apps → Manage apps → select the audio app → Battery saver → select No restrictions. Also enable Autostart where available.

Does screen-off playback save battery?

Turning off the display generally saves power, but streaming, Bluetooth, and audio processing still consume battery. Actual savings vary by device and usage.

Can podcasts continue playing with the phone locked?

Yes, when the podcast app supports background playback.

Why are lock-screen media controls missing?

This can be due to notification settings, lock-screen notification permissions, battery restrictions, or app compatibility. See the section above for fixes.

Can every Android app play audio with the screen off?

No. It depends on app support, source restrictions, and Android settings. Not all apps or media sources support screen-off playback.

Final Thoughts

Screen-off audio is a practical Android feature that works well when the app, settings, and permissions are aligned. Start with a compatible app, check your battery and notification settings, and use the troubleshooting checklist to resolve common issues.

One action to try today: Open a supported audio app, start permitted audio, confirm that the media notification appears, lock the screen, and test playback for 30 seconds. If it works, you have successfully set up screen-off listening. If not, work through the checklist above — one step at a time.

Remember to respect the terms of the apps and services you use. Screen-off playback is a feature of Android, not a way to bypass content restrictions or platform limitations.
