1. Development status

Salvera is in development. Public availability, final features and pricing have not been announced on this website. These pre-release terms supplement the NextFlow Apps general terms; the terms applicable to a testing or released build must be provided with that build.

2. Medication tracking limitations

Salvera is intended to organize information. It does not diagnose a condition, prescribe medication, determine an appropriate dose or decide whether a medicine can be split. Follow the directions provided by your qualified healthcare professional and medicine label. Do not change a prescribed regimen based on an app estimate.

3. Reminders and stock forecasts

Notifications can be delayed or missed because of permissions, device power, battery settings or software behavior. Inventory and refill forecasts are estimates based on the information entered and use recorded. They are not proof that a dose was taken, a medicine remains in stock or a refill has been arranged.

4. Patient and caregiver responsibilities

Keep medication names, quantities, schedules and stock entries accurate. Share only information you have authority to share. A caregiver should act only within consent and permissions granted by the patient; access to a care tool does not create medical authority.

5. Accounts, sharing and deletion

Protect your account and device access. Do not forward private invitations or session information. Review the build-specific controls for caregiver permissions, access revocation and optional integrations. Use the account and data deletion request route for help; final retention and deletion terms require verification.

6. No clinical or pharmacy service

This website does not offer emergency monitoring, diagnosis, prescriptions, pharmacy ordering, delivery or delivery tracking. Proposed pharmacy integrations are not active services or confirmed partnerships. Salvera is not a substitute for clinical care or an emergency response service.

7. Contact and release terms

Developer: NextFlow Apps. Contact: support@nextflow-apps.com. No Salvera subscription is offered here. Any billing terms must be disclosed with the relevant offering. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
