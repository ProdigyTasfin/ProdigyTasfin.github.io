Designed around daily life

The care routines Salvera is being built around.

The following describes the development scope, not a verified list of released features. Availability may change during development and testing.

01

Medication scheduling

A planned place for medication names, schedules and dose records, using the regimen you have been given.

02

Flexible dosage records

The development scope includes half-tablet and multiple-unit entries. Recording a quantity does not determine whether a medicine should be divided or how much you should take.

03

Stock and refill planning

Planned inventory estimates and configurable refill reminders, including 14-day and 21-day thresholds. Estimates depend on accurate entries and actual use.

04

Patient and caregiver coordination

A Care Circle experience designed around invitations, patient consent and permission-limited care activities.

05

Daily check-ins

A planned way to keep track of everyday check-ins alongside medication routines. It is not an emergency monitoring service.

06

Optional Health Connect

Health Connect is part of the planned integration scope. Supported data types and availability must be documented for the released build.

07

On-device reminders

Offline medication reminders are part of the design where supported. Android permissions, device state and battery restrictions can affect delivery.

08

Care on your terms

Choose a personal-health experience, a caregiver experience, or both. Final access and synchronization behavior will be described with release information.

Your role, your routine

Three ways to take part.

Different responsibilities, with a clear distinction between your information and someone else's.

09

Manage My Health

The personal experience is intended for people organizing their own medication schedules and daily care routines.

10

Support Someone I Care For

The caregiver experience is intended for trusted people coordinating only the care activities the patient permits.

11

Both

A combined experience is intended for people managing their own health while supporting family members.

From setup to everyday use

A straightforward care workflow.

The intended flow, subject to the capabilities of the available build.

  1. Set up medications: enter names, prescribed quantities and schedules accurately.
  2. Review reminders: confirm the time zone and available notification permissions, then test delivery.
  3. Track stock: record starting quantities and actual use so estimates have a useful basis.
  4. Plan refills: review the selected reminder threshold and arrange refills directly with your pharmacy.
  5. Invite a caregiver when appropriate: review what is shared and grant only the care access you want.

Understand sharing before you use it.

Medication information is sensitive. The app's final privacy information must distinguish device-local records from selected synchronized information, explain account security, and describe caregiver consent and revocation.

A care organizer, not a clinical service.

Salvera does not provide diagnosis or prescriptions. Reminders and inventory forecasts are supporting tools, not guarantees. No emergency monitoring or pharmacy delivery service is offered here.

Questions before getting started.

Can I download Salvera now?

No public download is offered on this website. Contact NextFlow Apps for current development or testing information.

Will medication notifications always arrive?

No reminder should be treated as guaranteed. Device power, notification permissions, battery restrictions and the installed build can affect delivery. Review the support checklist and keep an appropriate independent reminder routine.

How accurate are stock and refill estimates?

Stock forecasts depend on the quantity entered, dose units, schedule and recorded use. Compare any estimate with the medicine you actually have. A reminder does not place a refill order.

What can a caregiver see or change?

Care Circle is being designed around patient consent and specific permissions. The exact access and revocation behavior must be confirmed for the build before sharing information.

Is Health Connect required?

The planned integration is optional. Availability, requested data types and storage behavior will be documented for the released build. Android lets you review Health Connect permissions in its settings.

Can I use Salvera on several devices?

Multi-device behavior is not yet confirmed here. Do not assume that every local record or reminder follows an account to another device; check with support before clearing or replacing your original device.

How do I request account deletion?

Use the account and data deletion page to contact support. There is no verified website deletion endpoint or confirmed in-app deletion sequence documented here.

Are appointments or pharmacy delivery available?

Appointment availability has not been verified. Pharmacy refill ordering, delivery and delivery tracking are proposed integration opportunities, not operational services offered on this website.
