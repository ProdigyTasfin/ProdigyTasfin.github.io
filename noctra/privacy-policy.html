Noctra Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Noctra collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Noctra mobile application.

Noctra is a dopamine detox, digital detox, focus, habit tracking, and attention recovery app designed to help users reduce distractions, manage focus sessions, track habits, and build healthier digital routines.

1. Information We Collect

Noctra is designed to collect only the information needed to provide core app features. The following may be stored locally on your device:

  • Focus session history, detox minutes, and recovery session progress
  • Habit tracking data, streaks, completed sessions, and failed sessions
  • App quota settings, boredom box settings, and selected recovery modes
  • Urge logs, reflection notes, mood selections, and personal progress entries
  • Selected atmospheres, ambience preferences, and personalization settings
  • Premium or subscription entitlement status provided through Google Play Billing

2. App Usage Access

Noctra may request Usage Access permission to help estimate app usage, support digital detox features, app quota awareness, and distraction tracking. This permission may allow the app to read information such as which apps are used and how long they are used.

This information is used only to provide Noctra’s app limit, screen time, detox, and focus-related features. Noctra does not sell this information.

3. Do Not Disturb Access

Noctra may request Do Not Disturb access to support Recovery Shield or focus protection features. If enabled, Noctra may help reduce calls and notifications during active focus or recovery sessions. This optional permission can be disabled at any time from Android settings.

4. Notifications

Noctra may request notification permission to send reminders, session updates, streak reminders, or focus-related alerts. You can disable notifications at any time from device settings.

5. Local Storage

Most Noctra data is stored locally on your device, including focus history, detox progress, habit data, reflection notes, settings, and app preferences. If you uninstall the app or clear app data, locally stored information may be permanently deleted.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

Noctra may offer premium subscriptions or in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. Noctra does not collect or store your credit card, debit card, bank, or payment account information. Subscription status may unlock Premium or Sanctuary features.

7. Advertising

Noctra may display ads, including rewarded ads, to support free features such as rewarded extensions or temporary unlocks. Advertising services such as Google AdMob may process device information, ad identifiers, approximate location, interaction data, or diagnostics under their own policies.

8. Analytics and Diagnostics

Noctra may use analytics or crash reporting tools to understand performance, fix bugs, improve stability, and improve user experience. This may include device type, app version, crash logs, performance information, and general usage events. Noctra does not use analytics to personally identify users.

9. How We Use Information

  • Provide focus, detox, habit tracking, and recovery features
  • Track progress, streaks, sessions, and usage patterns
  • Support app limits, boredom box, and digital detox tools
  • Enable Premium and Sanctuary features
  • Improve performance, stability, and user experience
  • Show ads or rewarded content where applicable
  • Respond to support or privacy requests

10. Data Sharing

Noctra does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google Play Billing, Google AdMob or advertising partners, and analytics or crash-reporting tools. These third-party services process information under their own privacy policies.

11. Data Security

Noctra uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device unless you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. You may reset or delete certain progress data inside the app where available. Google Play handles retention of subscription and payment-related records under its own policies.

13. Children’s Privacy

Noctra is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the app.

14. Medical Disclaimer

Noctra is a productivity, focus, habit tracking, and digital wellness tool. It is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Seek help from a qualified professional for addiction, mental health concerns, or any serious condition.

15. Your Choices

  • Disable app permissions from Android settings
  • Disable notifications from device settings
  • Revoke Usage Access permission
  • Revoke Do Not Disturb access
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app
  • Contact us for privacy-related questions

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated Last updated date. Continued use of Noctra after changes means you accept the updated policy.

17. Contact Us
