Adult Shield

Extra Protection When You Need It

Adult Shield uses Android VpnService as a DNS-based protection layer for supported adult and malicious domains while the feature is active. It is separate from app blocking, which uses Accessibility to detect selected apps.

It is not a general-purpose VPN, does not change your public IP address or location, and does not tunnel normal web, video or app traffic. DNS filtering has limits and cannot block every item on mixed-content platforms.

Privacy, plainly: Adult Shield processes DNS requests needed for filtering. Noctra does not build a browsing-history profile, and most app data remains on your device. Third-party services may process limited diagnostics, billing or advertising data as described in the policy.

Know before you join

November Reset Rules

These are campaign commitments for adults 18+, not medical instructions.

01

Commit for 30 days

Keep the boundaries you choose throughout the full Reset. The goal is consistency, not a claim of perfection.

02

Keep Adult Shield active

Where required by the campaign, Adult Shield should remain enabled during the active Reset.

03

Do not replace its VPN

Android generally allows one VPN service at a time. Another VPN can take over the authorization Adult Shield needs, so using one conflicts with this campaign rule.

04

Do not move to a PC to bypass it

Noctra cannot monitor or control a computer. This is a personal commitment not to use another device specifically to bypass your chosen boundaries.

About local progress: Uninstalling Noctra or clearing its app data permanently deletes locally stored progress. The published campaign documentation does not state a separate account-switching penalty. After completion, app blocking, progress tools and Adult Shield remain available for ongoing use, subject to your plan.

No hidden permission story

Privacy & Trust

Permissions are tied to features you choose. You can revoke them in Android Settings, though the related protection will stop working.

Privacy PolicyTerms & ConditionsData deletion
Accessibility

Detects when a user-selected app opens so Noctra can apply your blocking rule. It is not used to read passwords, messages, payment details or typed text.

VpnService

Lets Adult Shield apply DNS-based filtering for supported domains. It is not a location-changing, general-purpose VPN.

Usage & storage

Usage Access supports app limits and distraction tracking. That usage data stays on-device. Most progress, settings and reflections are also stored locally.

Outside the device

Google Play handles payments. AdMob and Firebase/Crashlytics may process limited advertising, device, diagnostic or performance data; Noctra does not sell personal information.

Questions, answered

November Reset FAQ

What is the Noctra November Reset?

It is Noctra’s adults-only 30-day November 2026 campaign: choose digital boundaries, use the relevant protection tools and keep your progress visible for the month.

Is Noctra free to download?

Yes. Noctra is free to download on compatible Android devices. Optional Premium plans unlock additional features.

What does the $11.11 Lifetime offer include?

The planned eligible-market offer unlocks Noctra Premium for the lifetime of the product, including adaptive on-device insights, advanced personalization, additional focus modes and priority support. Features may evolve; confirm the current offer in Google Play before purchase.

What happens if I uninstall Noctra during the Reset?

Progress is stored locally. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes that locally stored information, including Reset progress.

Does Noctra see or store my browsing history?

Adult Shield handles DNS requests needed to filter supported domains, but Noctra does not build a browsing-history profile. Most app data stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for third-party diagnostics, ads and billing disclosures.

How does Adult Shield work?

It uses Android VpnService for DNS-based filtering of supported adult and malicious domains. It does not tunnel all traffic, change your location or guarantee coverage of every page or mixed-content platform.

Can I still use Noctra after November?

Yes. App limits, selected-app blocking, progress tools and Adult Shield can support digital boundaries throughout the year.

Is Noctra available on iPhone?

Noctra is currently available for Android through Google Play. iPhone support is not currently advertised.
