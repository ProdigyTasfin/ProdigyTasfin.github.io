Privacy & trust

Powerful permissions, explained plainly.

Noctra asks for feature-specific Android access only when you choose the related protection. You can revoke permissions in Android Settings, but that feature will stop working.

Accessibility

Used to detect when an app you selected opens so Noctra can apply your blocking or focus rule. Noctra does not use Accessibility to read passwords, private messages, payment details, or text you type.

Android VpnService

Used by Adult Shield to apply DNS-based filtering. It is not a general-purpose VPN, does not change your public IP or location, and does not tunnel normal web, video, or app traffic.

What filtering can—and cannot—do

Adult Shield filters supported domains. Browser Secure DNS, Android Private DNS, another VPN, and network configuration may affect it. It cannot guarantee coverage of every page or mixed-content platform.

Data handling

Usage data, progress, settings, and reflections are primarily stored on-device. Google Play handles billing; AdMob and Firebase/Crashlytics may process limited advertising, device, diagnostic, or performance data as disclosed in the policy.

November Reset FAQ

What is No Nut November 2026?

No Nut November, often shortened to NNN, is an informal online challenge in which adults choose a personal abstinence commitment for November. There is no universal official rulebook, and it is not medical guidance.

What is Noctra's November Reset?

It is an adults-only, fixed 30-day campaign that brings Noctra's selected-app blocking, Adult Shield, focus tools, and progress tracking into one November commitment.

Can Noctra help with No Goon November or No Fap November?

Adults can use Noctra to add digital boundaries to a No Goon November, No Gooning November, No Fap November, or personal no-porn commitment. The app provides tools; it does not guarantee an outcome.

Can Noctra block porn on Android?

Noctra's Adult Shield is a porn-blocker feature that can filter supported adult domains at the DNS level on Android. It is not a guarantee that every adult item or mixed-content platform will be blocked.

How does Adult Shield block adult websites?

It uses Android VpnService to route the DNS requests needed for filtering. It blocks supported adult and malicious domains without acting as a location-changing, general-purpose VPN.

Does Adult Shield work with incognito browsing?

DNS filtering is device-level rather than a browser-history feature, so supported-domain filtering can still apply when a browser uses incognito mode. Browser Secure DNS, Android Private DNS, another VPN, or network configuration can affect coverage.

What happens if I uninstall Noctra during the Reset?

Progress is stored locally. Uninstalling Noctra or clearing its app data permanently deletes that local information, including Reset progress.

Does Noctra monitor my browsing history?

Adult Shield processes the DNS requests needed for filtering, but Noctra does not build a browsing-history profile. Most Noctra data remains on the device; limited billing, advertising, and diagnostic data may be processed by disclosed third parties.

Is the November Reset for adults only?

Yes. Noctra's November 2026 campaign is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

What does the $11.11 Lifetime offer include?

In eligible markets from November 1 through November 30, 2026, $11.11 is a one-time Google Play purchase for Noctra Lifetime Premium access for the supported lifetime of the product. Google Play shows final local pricing and availability before purchase.

Can I use Noctra after November?

Yes. Noctra's app limits, selected-app blocking, Adult Shield, progress, and focus tools are designed for use beyond the campaign. Access to Premium features depends on your plan or purchase.
