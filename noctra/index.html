Focus guidance that learns from your patterns

When enabled, Noctra can learn from your usage history on your device and offer more helpful suggestions over time.

How it works: Noctra analyzes local usage patterns — such as typical session duration, repeated reopenings, high‑risk usage periods, late‑night patterns, and intervention choices — to offer more relevant guidance as you continue using the app.

Important: Personalization becomes more useful as enough history is collected. Noctra doesn't pretend to know you immediately. You can disable or reset adaptive learning at any time.

How Noctra uses Android Accessibility

When you enable app blocking, Noctra uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect when an app you selected is opened so it can apply the blocking or focus rule you configured.

You choose which apps to control. You must explicitly enable Accessibility in Android Settings. The permission can be disabled at any time.

Noctra does not use Accessibility to read passwords, payment information, private messages, or text you type.
