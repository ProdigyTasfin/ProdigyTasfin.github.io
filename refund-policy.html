Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to all purchases made through Next Flow Apps, including subscriptions and Lifetime Premium access.

Plain‑English summary

If you buy something from us and it doesn't work as described, or if there's a technical problem, we'll help. We encourage you to contact us promptly (ideally within 48 hours) so we can resolve issues quickly. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by this policy. For Lifetime purchases, please read the Lifetime Premium section carefully — it explains what we mean by "Lifetime" and what happens if the service ends.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to all purchases made through Next Flow Apps, including but not limited to:

  • Subscriptions — recurring payments for Premium access (e.g., monthly, yearly).
  • Lifetime Premium — one‑time payments for ongoing Premium access.
  • Other one‑time purchases — such as individual feature unlocks where available.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. This policy is designed to be fair and transparent, and it operates alongside Google Play's own refund policies and applicable consumer protection laws.

2. Google Play Purchases

All in‑app purchases are processed by Google Play. This means:

  • Next Flow Apps does not receive or store your full payment card details, passwords, or Google account credentials.
  • Google Play may process refunds independently under their own policies, particularly within approximately 48 hours of purchase.
  • If Google Play processes a refund for a purchase, your Premium entitlement may be revoked automatically.
  • You cannot receive both a refund from Google Play and a refund from Next Flow Apps for the same purchase.

If you have a purchase or billing issue, we encourage you to check Google Play's refund help page first, especially for recent purchases.

3. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us via email. We encourage you to do so promptly — ideally within 48 hours of the purchase — as this allows us to resolve issues quickly and aligns with Google Play's standard refund processing.

Important: The 48‑hour period is a recommendation for prompt contact, not a strict legal cutoff. Your statutory consumer rights may apply regardless of when you contact us. If you contact us after 48 hours, we will still consider your request fairly and in accordance with applicable law.

How to submit a refund request

  1. Send an email to nextflowapps@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Refund Request – [App Name] (e.g., "Refund Request – HushFlow").
  3. Include the following information:
  • Your order number – this is the GPA order number that starts with GPA. (e.g., GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567)
  • App name (HushFlow, Noctra, or Temvica)
  • Purchase type (Subscription or Lifetime)
  • Reason for refund (brief description of the issue)
  • Date and time of purchase (as shown in your Google Play receipt)

Note: We do not request your full name, Google password, payment card details, or other sensitive personal information.

For assistance locating your GPA order number, please refer to the Google Play support article on finding order numbers.

4. When a Refund or Other Remedy May Be Available

You may be eligible for a refund or other remedy in the following circumstances:

a) Technical Defects or Non‑Functionality

  • The purchased feature does not work as described in the app listing or in‑app descriptions.
  • The app experiences persistent crashes or errors that prevent the purchased feature from being used.
  • We have not been able to resolve the issue within a reasonable time after being notified.

b) Non‑Receipt of Purchase

  • You were charged for a subscription or purchase but the Premium features were not unlocked in the app.
  • In this case, please contact us — we can often resolve the issue without requiring a refund.

c) Duplicate or Accidental Purchase

  • You accidentally purchased the same subscription twice.
  • You purchased a subscription when you intended to purchase a Lifetime licence, or vice versa.

d) Unauthorised Purchase

  • You believe a purchase was made without your authorisation. In this case, please also contact Google Play Support.
Important: Where a refund is not required by law or by Google Play policy, we exercise our discretion fairly. We reserve the right to deny requests that are abusive, fraudulent, or made in bad faith.

5. Subscriptions

Subscription payments are processed by Google Play. The following points are important to understand:

  • Cancellation: You may cancel your subscription at any time via your Google Play account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically entitle you to a refund for past charges.
  • Automatic renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew unless you cancel before the renewal date. Google Play handles this process, and their standard cancellation window applies.
  • Refunds for renewals: If you were charged for a renewal that you did not want, you should contact Google Play Support directly. Next Flow Apps may assist at our discretion in cases of clear error.
  • Partial refunds: If a subscription is defective, we may consider a partial refund proportional to the unused period, where reasonably practicable.

For assistance with cancelling your subscription, please refer to Google Play's subscription cancellation guide.

6. Lifetime Premium

What "Lifetime" means: A Lifetime Premium purchase is a one‑time payment that provides Premium access for the commercially supported life of the relevant app or service.

"Commercially supported" means the period during which Next Flow Apps actively maintains and updates the product. This does not mean the biological lifetime of the user or the developer, nor does it mean the app will operate forever without change.

What Lifetime does not mean:

  • It is not a guarantee that the app will operate forever, or that Next Flow Apps will exist forever.
  • It is not a guarantee that every feature available today will remain unchanged forever.
  • It is not a guarantee that servers or third‑party services will operate forever.

Existing Lifetime purchasers: This policy applies to purchases made from August 2026 onward. For Lifetime purchases made before this date, your core entitlement remains as described at the time of your purchase. We are committed to treating all Lifetime purchasers fairly and will not materially reduce existing entitlements.

Feature changes: We reserve the right to update, improve, or modify Premium features over time. If we remove a core feature that was central to the Lifetime offer, we will communicate this and consider proportionate remedies. Minor updates, design changes, or feature replacements do not automatically entitle you to a refund.

Proportional remedies: If a Lifetime product is discontinued or a core feature is permanently removed, we will consider the total useful service already received, the severity of the lost functionality, and the purchase price. Remedies may include partial refunds, alternative access, or other reasonable solutions.

7. Feature Changes and Technical Problems

We distinguish between different types of changes and issues:

  • Normal updates and redesigns: These are part of ongoing app development and do not entitle you to a refund.
  • Replacement with substantially equivalent functionality: If a feature is replaced with a similar alternative, this generally does not constitute a defect.
  • Temporary outages: Short-term technical issues, server maintenance, or third‑party service interruptions may occur. We will make reasonable efforts to restore functionality.
  • Third‑party dependency failure: If a core feature depends on a service we do not control, we will make reasonable efforts to restore or replace it. If replacement is not possible, we will consider proportionate remedies.
  • Material loss of a core paid feature: If a feature that was central to the purchase is permanently removed, you may be entitled to a proportionate remedy.
  • Permanent service discontinuation: See Section 8 below.

8. Product Discontinuation / End of Service

If we ever need to discontinue an app or service, we will make reasonable efforts to provide advance notice where reasonably possible.

Our approach will include:

  • Communication through in‑app notices, the website, or other available channels.
  • Consideration of alternatives or workarounds where practical.
  • Evaluation of appropriate remedies based on:
    • The amount of useful service already received
    • How recently the purchase was made
    • The severity of the lost functionality
    • Whether the entire product or only one feature is affected
    • Applicable statutory rights
    • Google Play requirements

Examples:

  • Shutdown after 1 year: May justify a significant proportional refund for Lifetime purchasers.
  • Shutdown after 5+ years: May justify a smaller remedy or none, depending on usage and context.
  • Shutdown due to external factors: (e.g., platform changes, API deprecation) — remedies will be considered fairly in context.
No automatic promises: We do not promise a refund in every scenario, nor do we promise that every Lifetime purchase will be fully refunded if the service eventually ends. However, we do promise to treat all users fairly and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Unauthorised, Duplicate and Accidental Purchases

  • Unauthorised purchases: If you believe a purchase was made without your authorisation, please contact Google Play Support immediately. Next Flow Apps will assist where we can, but Google Play has direct access to the purchase records and billing systems.
  • Duplicate charges: If you were charged twice for the same purchase, contact us and we will investigate and assist.
  • Accidental purchases: If you accidentally made a purchase, please contact us promptly. We will consider your request fairly.

10. Refund Abuse and Duplicate Reimbursement

Next Flow Apps reserves the right to deny refund requests where we have reasonable grounds to believe the request is fraudulent, abusive, or made in bad faith.

Examples may include:

  • Frequent refund requests without valid reasons
  • Duplicate refund attempts (e.g., requesting a refund from both Google Play and Next Flow Apps)
  • Falsified order information
  • Attempting to retain Premium access after receiving a refund

If a refund is granted for a purchase, your Premium entitlement may be revoked.

11. Effect of a Refund on Premium Access

If you receive a refund for a purchase, your access to the associated Premium features may be revoked. This applies to:

  • Subscriptions that are refunded
  • Lifetime Premium purchases that are refunded
  • Any other purchase that is refunded

You cannot receive a refund and continue to retain the Premium entitlement.

12. Consumer Rights by Law

This policy does not override your legal rights.

Nothing in this Refund Policy excludes, restricts, or overrides consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded or restricted under applicable law.

European Union / EEA

Under EU consumer law, you may have a right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days of the purchase date for digital content. However, by purchasing through Google Play, you may have expressly consented to the immediate delivery of the digital content (Premium features). Where this applies, you acknowledge that you may lose your right to withdraw once the features have been unlocked.

United Kingdom

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, you have the right to a refund if digital content is not of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, or as described. Your statutory rights are not affected by this policy.

Australia

Our goods and services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and for compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if they fail to be of acceptable quality.

Other jurisdictions

We will honour any rights you have under applicable local consumer protection laws. If you are unsure about your rights, please contact your local consumer protection authority.

Jurisdictional note: This policy is written for an international audience. Some provisions may be more restrictive or more generous depending on your location. Where there is a conflict between this policy and your statutory consumer rights, your statutory rights prevail.

13. Existing Purchases and Policy Changes

We distinguish between different types of policy changes:

  • Administrative or clarifying updates: These may apply to all users and do not materially affect existing purchases.
  • Legally required updates: These may be necessary to comply with new laws or regulations.
  • Material changes affecting existing purchases: If we make a material change that significantly affects the value or terms of an existing purchase (particularly Lifetime purchases), we will:
    • Provide reasonable notice where possible
    • Consider whether the change is fair to existing purchasers
    • Not apply material changes retroactively in a way that unfairly reduces what was already purchased

This policy was last updated on August 2026. It applies to all purchases made from this date onward. For purchases made earlier, the policy in effect at the time of purchase generally applies, subject to any legally required updates.

14. Refund Processing

Once we approve your refund request:

  1. We will issue a refund request through Google Play Billing where applicable.
  2. Google Play will process the refund and credit the amount to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.
  3. You will receive a confirmation email from Google Play once the refund has been processed.
  4. Please allow 3‑10 business days for the refund to appear in your account (timing depends on your bank or payment provider).

Important: Next Flow Apps does not have direct access to your payment information. All transactions are handled securely by Google Play Billing. Refunds are subject to Google's own policies and procedures.
