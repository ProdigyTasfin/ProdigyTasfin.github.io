Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to all purchases made through Next Flow Apps, including subscriptions and Lifetime Premium access.

Plain‑English summary

If you buy something from us and it doesn't work as described, or if there's a technical problem, we'll help. We encourage you to contact us promptly (ideally within 48 hours) so we can resolve issues quickly. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by this policy. For Lifetime purchases, please read the Lifetime Premium section carefully — it explains what we mean by "Lifetime" and what happens if the service ends.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to all purchases made through Next Flow Apps, including but not limited to:

  • Subscriptions — recurring payments for Premium access (e.g., monthly, yearly).
  • Lifetime Premium — one‑time payments for ongoing Premium access.
  • Other one‑time purchases — such as individual feature unlocks where available.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. This policy is designed to be fair and transparent, and it operates alongside Google Play's own refund policies and applicable consumer protection laws.

2. Google Play Purchases

All in‑app purchases are processed by Google Play. This means:

  • Next Flow Apps does not receive or store your full payment card details, passwords, or Google account credentials.
  • Google Play may process refund requests independently under its own policies. For recent purchases, you may be able to request a refund directly through Google Play.
  • If Google Play processes a refund for a purchase, your Premium entitlement may be revoked automatically.
  • You cannot receive both a refund from Google Play and a refund from Next Flow Apps for the same purchase.

If you have a purchase or billing issue, we encourage you to check Google Play's refund help page first, especially for recent purchases.

3. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us via email. We encourage you to do so promptly — ideally within 48 hours of the purchase — as this allows us to resolve issues quickly and aligns with Google Play's standard refund processing.

  • Cancellation: You may cancel your subscription at any time via your Google Play account settings. Subscriptions automatically renew according to the billing terms displayed by Google Play unless cancelled. Cancellation generally stops future renewals but does not itself issue a refund for the current billing period, except where otherwise provided by Google Play or applicable law.
    • Important: The 48-hour period does not affect any refund or consumer rights you may have under applicable law that cannot legally be waived. If you contact us after 48 hours, we will still consider your request fairly and in accordance with applicable law.

    How to submit a refund request

    1. Send an email to nextflowapps@gmail.com.
    2. Use the subject line: Refund Request – [App Name] (e.g., "Refund Request – HushFlow").
    3. Include the following information:
    • Your order number – this is the GPA order number that starts with GPA. (e.g., GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567)
    • App name (HushFlow, Noctra, or Temvica)
    • Purchase type (Subscription or Lifetime)
    • Reason for refund (brief description of the issue)
    • Date and time of purchase (as shown in your Google Play receipt)
    Important privacy note: Next Flow Apps will never ask for your Google password, full payment-card number, or authentication codes for a refund request.

    For assistance locating your GPA order number, please refer to the Google Play support article on finding order numbers.

    4. When a Refund or Other Remedy May Be Available

    You may be eligible for a refund or other remedy in the following circumstances:

    a) Technical Defects or Non‑Functionality

    • The purchased feature does not work as described in the app listing or in‑app descriptions.
    • The app experiences persistent crashes or errors that prevent the purchased feature from being used.
    • We have not been able to resolve the issue within a reasonable time after being notified.

    b) Non‑Receipt of Purchase

    • You were charged for a subscription or purchase but the Premium features were not unlocked in the app.
    • In this case, please contact us — we can often resolve the issue without requiring a refund.

    c) Duplicate or Accidental Purchase

    • You accidentally purchased the same subscription twice.
    • You purchased a subscription when you intended to purchase a Lifetime licence, or vice versa.

    d) Unauthorised Purchase

    • You believe a purchase was made without your authorisation. In this case, please also contact Google Play Support.
    Important: Refund requests are assessed under this Policy, applicable Google Play requirements, the circumstances of the purchase, and applicable law. We reserve the right to deny requests that are abusive, fraudulent, or made in bad faith.

    5. Subscriptions

    Subscription payments are processed by Google Play. The following points are important to understand:

    • Cancellation: You may cancel your subscription at any time via your Google Play account settings. Subscriptions automatically renew according to the billing terms displayed by Google Play unless cancelled. Cancellation generally stops future renewals but does not itself issue a refund for the current billing period, except where otherwise provided by Google Play or applicable law.
    • Automatic renewal: You can manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play. To avoid being charged for a renewal, please cancel before the renewal date.
    • Refunds for renewals: If you were charged for a renewal that you did not want, you should contact Google Play Support directly. Next Flow Apps may assist at our discretion in cases of clear error.
    • Partial refunds: If a subscription is defective, we may consider a partial refund proportional to the unused period, where reasonably practicable.

    For assistance with cancelling your subscription, please refer to Google Play's subscription cancellation guide.

    6. Lifetime Premium

    What "Lifetime" means for future purchases: A Lifetime Premium purchase is a one-time payment that provides Premium access for the supported lifetime of the applicable product. It is not a subscription and does not mean that the product, company, infrastructure, third-party services, or every individual feature will necessarily operate indefinitely.

    "Supported lifetime" means the period during which Next Flow Apps continues to operate, maintain, or support the relevant app or service as an active product.

    What Lifetime does not mean:

    • It is not a guarantee that the app or service will operate forever.
    • It is not a guarantee that Next Flow Apps will exist or operate indefinitely.
    • It is not a guarantee that every feature available at the time of purchase will remain unchanged forever.
    • It is not a guarantee that third-party platforms, APIs, servers, operating systems, infrastructure, or other dependencies will remain available indefinitely.
    • It does not require any individual developer to personally maintain the product indefinitely.

    Features may evolve, be redesigned, replaced, or discontinued as the product develops, provided that we act reasonably and in accordance with applicable consumer law.

    Existing Lifetime purchasers: This definition does not retroactively reduce rights or entitlements attached to Lifetime purchases completed before this definition took effect. Earlier purchases remain subject to the terms, representations, and applicable rights associated with the purchase at the time it was made, together with any non-waivable rights provided by applicable law.

    If the product or a core paid feature is discontinued: We will consider the circumstances fairly, including how recently the purchase was made, the useful service already received, the nature and severity of the lost functionality, available alternatives, the original purchase price, and applicable law. Where appropriate, a remedy may include alternative access, replacement functionality, a partial refund, a full refund where legally required, or another reasonable solution.

    7. Feature Changes and Technical Problems

    We distinguish between different types of changes and issues:

    • Normal updates and redesigns: These are part of ongoing app development and do not entitle you to a refund.
    • Replacement with substantially equivalent functionality: If a feature is replaced with a similar alternative, this generally does not constitute a defect.
    • Temporary outages: Short-term technical issues, server maintenance, or third‑party service interruptions may occur. We will make reasonable efforts to restore functionality.
    • Third‑party dependency failure: If a core feature depends on a service we do not control, we will make reasonable efforts to restore or replace it. If replacement is not possible, we will consider proportionate remedies.
    • Material loss of a core paid feature: If a feature that was central to the purchase is permanently removed, you may be entitled to a proportionate remedy.
    • Permanent service discontinuation: See Section 8 below.

    8. Product Discontinuation / End of Service

    Technology, platforms, operating systems, third-party services, business conditions, legal requirements, or other circumstances may eventually make it necessary to discontinue an app, service, or particular feature.

    If Next Flow Apps decides to permanently discontinue an app or service, we will make reasonable efforts to provide advance notice where reasonably practicable.

    Where appropriate and reasonably practicable, our approach may include:

    • Providing notice through the app, our website, email, or other available communication channels.
    • Providing reasonable time for users to prepare for the discontinuation where circumstances permit.
    • Considering alternative functionality, migration options, replacement services, or workarounds where practical.
    • Considering appropriate remedies for affected paid users where required or appropriate.

    Any remedy will depend on the individual circumstances, including how recently the purchase was made, the amount of useful service already received, the nature of the discontinued functionality, whether the entire product or only part of it is affected, available alternatives, Google Play requirements, and applicable consumer law.

    No automatic refund promise: The eventual discontinuation of a product does not automatically guarantee a full refund of every previous purchase. However, this does not limit any refund, repair, replacement, price-reduction, cancellation, or other remedy that you are legally entitled to under applicable consumer law. We will assess affected purchases fairly based on their circumstances.

    9. Unauthorised, Duplicate and Accidental Purchases

    • Unauthorised purchases: If you believe a purchase was made without your authorisation, please contact Google Play Support immediately. Next Flow Apps will assist where we can, but Google Play has direct access to the purchase records and billing systems.
    • Duplicate charges: If you were charged twice for the same purchase, contact us and we will investigate and assist.
    • Accidental purchases: If you accidentally made a purchase, please contact us promptly. We will consider your request fairly.

    10. Refund Abuse and Duplicate Reimbursement

    Next Flow Apps reserves the right to deny refund requests where we have reasonable grounds to believe the request is fraudulent, abusive, or made in bad faith.

    Examples may include:

    • Frequent refund requests without valid reasons
    • Duplicate refund attempts (e.g., requesting a refund from both Google Play and Next Flow Apps)
    • Falsified order information
    • Attempting to retain Premium access after receiving a refund

    If a refund is granted for a purchase, your Premium entitlement may be revoked.

    11. Effect of a Refund on Premium Access

    If you receive a refund for a purchase, your access to the associated Premium features may be revoked. This applies to:

    • Subscriptions that are refunded
    • Lifetime Premium purchases that are refunded
    • Any other purchase that is refunded

    You cannot receive a refund and continue to retain the Premium entitlement.

    For partial refunds, entitlement may be adjusted proportionately where technically feasible and legally appropriate.
